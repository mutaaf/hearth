For the house full of slow smart TVs

One remote for every TV in the house.

Hearth finds the LG and Samsung TVs on your Wi-Fi and turns every phone into a fast, ad-free remote. No accounts, no cloud. It runs at home, on your network.

Try the demo Install in one command ↓

the house

01

Find

Hearth listens on your Wi-Fi and finds every LG webOS and Samsung Tizen TV. No model numbers, no IP addresses.

02

Pair

Each TV asks once: press Allow with its own remote. After that, every phone in the house can drive it.

03

Control

Swipe-pad, volume, apps, inputs, “Dinner’s ready!” on screen, and one button that turns every TV in the house off.

Install

One command on a computer that stays on.

A Mac mini, an old laptop, a Raspberry Pi. You need Node.js 20.19+. Then:

$ git clone https://github.com/mutaaf/hearth
$ cd hearth && npm run up

🔥 Hearth is ready · scan the QR code with your phone

Why doesn’t it just run on this website?

On purpose. Browsers, rightly, won’t let a website reach the devices on your home network. Hearth runs inside your house, so your TVs, pairing keys and viewing habits never leave it. The demo uses pretend TVs.

Old TV, new life

Four years in, smart TVs drown in ads, tracking and bloat. The tune-up guide fixes that in about 20 minutes per TV: settings, whole-house ad blocking, a factory reset done right, LG homebrew, or a cheap box that turns the TV into a plain screen.
